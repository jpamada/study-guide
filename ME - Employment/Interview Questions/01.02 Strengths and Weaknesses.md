#### Make your strengths also your weakness

**Strengths**
"My strength is that I am detail-oriented."
"When taking notes I want it all to be organized."
"When programming I like to make my code clean and readable. That's why I also like refactoring."
"My other strength is that I am willing to learn (talk about still learning after graduation and love for programming)."

**Weaknesses**
"My strengths is also my weakness"
"Because I'm detail-oriented it makes me overthink about a lot of things."
"In terms of love of learning, I often study too much to the point of ignoring my own health"
