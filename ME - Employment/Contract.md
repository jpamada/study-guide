#### Examples

**Regular Employment**
The most stable type of employment.

**Fixed-Term Employment**
You are hired for a specific period of time.

**Probationary Employment**
You are hired for an evaluation period before becoming a regular employee. Probationary employment is commonly up to six months.

**What is Project-Based Job**
A temporary employment arrangement where a worker is hired to complete a specific task, goal, or assignment with a defined scope and timeline.

Cons
- Less Job Security
- Different Benefits
- Possible Pressure from Deadlines
