#### Examples

**Overtime Pay**
Working beyond your normal 8 hours. Generally requires additional overtime compensation under applicable rules.

**Night Shift Differential (NSD)**
10 PM – 6 AM at least 10% additional pay for qualifying hours.

**Allowance**
Additional amounts provided by an employer on top of your basic salary.

**Bonuses**
Extra pay given by the employer, often based on company policy, performance, or special occasions.

**Incentive**
Usually extra compensation given when you achieve a specific target or performance goal.
