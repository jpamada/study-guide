#### Examples

**[[Inheritance]]**
Allows a class to inherit properties and methods from another class.
 
**[[Encapsulation]]**
Protects object's internal data and controlling how that data is accessed or modified.

**[[Abstraction]]**
Hides complex logic. Similar to inheritance but can use policy.

**[[Polymorphism]]**
Allows the same method to behave differently depending on the object.
