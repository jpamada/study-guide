#### Definition

**[[Hoisting]]**
`var` can be used anywhere in its scope even if it's declaration isn't reached yet.

___
#### Related Concepts

**[[Temporal Dead Zone]]**
When entering a scope where a let/const variable exists and reaching its declaration.
