#### Examples

**Paid Leave**
allowed to take time off from work while still receiving your pay.

**Sick Leave**
Sick leave allows you to take time off when you are sick or unable to work.

**Vacation Leave**
Vacation leave allows you to take time off.

**Health Insurance**
PhilHealth helps cover certain healthcare services according to its benefit packages and rules.

**Health Maintenance Organization (HMO)**
A private health benefit provided by an employer or purchased individually.
